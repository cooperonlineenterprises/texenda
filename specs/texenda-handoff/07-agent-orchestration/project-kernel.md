# Project kernel — always read

**Texenda implementation handoff 1.1.0 · 2026-09-05 · NORMATIVE**

MUST/MUST NOT are binding; SHOULD requires a recorded reason to depart; MAY permits but does not commit scope. Source: Accepted interaction architecture and complete-handoff directive. Apply [package authority](../DECISION-STATUS.md).


Texenda is an email-led audience and communication platform for solopreneurs and teams of five or fewer. It maintains permissioned relationships through relevant, restrained communication. It is not a CRM/CDP/helpdesk/ads/ecommerce/CMS/sales-engagement/general agent suite.

The authoritative model lives in scoped PostgreSQL domain records and explicit application commands. Payload owns authentication/draft/admin machinery, not activated recipient policy. Visual UI, voice, chat, built-in AI, APIs and computer-use invoke the same commands. Shared identity cannot transfer consent. Reachability, identity proof, permission and preference are separate. Recipient Withdrawal and operational Suppression are separate. Unknown evidence cannot become permissive evidence.

One versioned workflow executor serves sequences and automations. Queues wake durable work. Snapshot candidates are immutable, but current denials and safety policy are rechecked. External effects use permanent semantic keys, independent recovery evidence and explicit unknown states. No blind provider/channel failover. Kit migrations transfer one authority; they do not dual-send.

AI proposes and explains; deterministic policy owns truth. Conversation is not state. Approval binds exact proposal/revisions/envelope, not casual speech. Agent principals use current bounded delegation; visible buttons are not additional authority. Manual operation remains complete without AI.

Working brand is Texenda; public clearance is outstanding. TypeScript/Payload/NextReact/PostgreSQL monolith and web/worker roles are baseline. Provider, queue, hosting, editor codec, model and speech choices are replaceable. New channels and specialized infrastructure require recorded triggers and qualification. Do not introduce speculative services.

Read `01-foundation/invariants.md` for all 30 invariants and `DECISION-STATUS.md` for authority and gates. The per-task context identifies exact owners. No broad rediscovery is needed. The development harness is local coordination—not a sandbox, production authorization, or evidence of application readiness.
